# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not individuals.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Put the health of the project and community ahead of personal conflict.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, and other conduct that would reasonably make someone feel unsafe or unwelcome are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, Pull Requests, reviews, and other public or private spaces where someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to [contact@gurwinderdevx.com](mailto:contact@gurwinderdevx.com). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and made in good faith. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern is prohibited.
